Getting connected

Use desktop Chrome or Edge on localhost. Close the PlatformIO serial monitor first so the browser can open the port. Power the T-Camera sender, allow five seconds for noise learning, then make a loud sound. The receiver reports its MAC and channel every five seconds.

Messages stay in this browser tab. No data is uploaded or stored after you close it.
